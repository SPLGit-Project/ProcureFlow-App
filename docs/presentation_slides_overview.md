### Slide 7: Closed-Loop Governance: Point-of-Entry Packaging & Pricing Enforcement
* **Category:** Requisition Integrity
* **Two Core Governance Mechanisms:**
  * **1. Carton Modulo & 1-Click Smart Rounding:** Evaluates $\text{quantityOrdered} \pmod{\text{cartonQty}} == 0$. Submission is blocked if carton rules are violated. An interactive 1-click button allows users to immediately round to the nearest carton multiple (e.g. *"Round to 5,040 units"*), eliminating vendor packaging rejections and saving procurement triage hours.
  * **2. Contract Price Master Enforcement:** Unit prices auto-populate from active contracted supplier catalogs. Manual price editing and $0.00 entries are blocked, preventing downstream invoice matching freezes in SAP Concur and protecting commercial margins.
* **Presenter Speaking Note (Aaron):**
  > *"Instead of discovering pricing errors or carton mismatches days later when an invoice is frozen, ProcureFlow solves them at the point of entry. The user gets an immediate 1-click solution, and finance receives an error-free transaction."*
